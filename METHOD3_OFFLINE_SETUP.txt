================================================================================
  SCHOOLHEAT - METHOD 3: OFFLINE USB TETHERING
  No WiFi? No Internet? No Problem!
================================================================================

WHAT IS THIS?
-------------
This method lets you share the app with judges even when the venue has:
- No WiFi
- No internet
- No mobile data
- Nothing!

You create your OWN network between your laptop and the judge's phone
using a USB cable.

SETUP (Android Phone)
---------------------
1. Connect your Android phone to the laptop with a USB cable
2. On your phone: Settings > Network & Internet > Hotspot & Tethering
3. Turn ON "USB Tethering"
4. Your laptop now has internet through your phone (or just a local network)

SETUP (iPhone)
--------------
1. Connect iPhone to laptop with USB cable
2. On iPhone: Settings > Personal Hotspot > Turn ON
3. On laptop: Network settings should show "iPhone USB"
4. If not, open iTunes or Finder and trust the device

START THE BRIDGE SERVER
-----------------------
Open Command Prompt in SchoolHeat_TUKLAS folder:

    python bridge_server.py --host 0.0.0.0 --port 5000

The --host 0.0.0.0 is IMPORTANT! It allows connections from other devices.

FIND YOUR LAPTOP'S IP ADDRESS
-----------------------------
In Command Prompt, run:

    ipconfig

Look for "IPv4 Address" under the adapter that says "Ethernet adapter"
or "Local Area Connection". It will be something like:

    192.168.137.1

This is the address judges type into their phone browser.

CONNECT THE APP
---------------
1. Open SchoolHeat app (or browser)
2. Go to Settings tab
3. Enter: http://192.168.137.1:5000 (use YOUR actual IP)
4. Click Connect
5. Status: "Online - Arduino Bridge"

JUDGES ACCESS THE APP
---------------------
1. Judge connects to your laptop's network (via USB tethering)
   OR judge is on the same local network created by USB tethering
2. Judge opens phone browser
3. Judge types: http://192.168.137.1:5000
4. Judge sees the app!
5. Judge can view live Arduino data!

IMPORTANT
---------
- The IP address (192.168.137.1) might be DIFFERENT on your laptop.
  Always check with "ipconfig" first.

- USB tethering creates a one-way connection: laptop gets internet from phone.
  But it ALSO creates a local network that the phone can use to reach the laptop.

- If this doesn't work, try the ngrok method instead (requires internet).

- Windows Firewall might block the connection. If judges can't connect:
  1. Open Windows Security > Firewall & Network Protection
  2. Click "Allow an app through firewall"
  3. Find "Python" and check both Private and Public boxes
  4. Or temporarily turn off firewall during demo
