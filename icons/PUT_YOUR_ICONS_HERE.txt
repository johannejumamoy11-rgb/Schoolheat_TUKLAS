Add these files to this folder:

  icon-192.png          - App icon, 192x192 pixels
  icon-512.png          - App icon, 512x512 pixels
  icon-192-maskable.png - Maskable icon variant (optional)
  icon-512-maskable.png - Maskable icon variant (optional)

The app will work without these, but PyInstaller may show a warning.
