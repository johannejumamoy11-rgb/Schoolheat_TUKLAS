About SchoolHeat

TUKLAS 2026 — Mahaplag National High School

SchoolHeat Ultimate

Version 3.0

A real-time heat index monitoring system designed for Mahaplag National High School. Built to protect students and faculty from heat-related illnesses by providing instant, location-specific heat index readings across the entire campus.

Real-Time Monitoring Arduino DHT22 sensor integration
Trend Analysis Visual charts and 7-day forecasting
Campus Map 29 location pins with hazard levels
Heat Index Formula Steadman-Rothfusz calculation
