WHAT IS GITHUB PAGES?
---------------------
GitHub Pages is a FREE service that hosts your website. Once you upload
your SchoolHeat files, you get a permanent URL like:

    https://yourusername.github.io/schoolheat/

This URL never changes. Judges can bookmark it. It works forever.

STEP 1: CREATE A GITHUB ACCOUNT (Free)
--------------------------------------
1. Go to https://github.com/signup
2. Enter your email, create password, choose username
3. Verify your email

STEP 2: CREATE A NEW REPOSITORY
-------------------------------
1. Click the green "+" button (top right) → "New repository"
2. Repository name: schoolheat
3. Make it PUBLIC
4. Click "Create repository"

STEP 3: UPLOAD YOUR FILES
-------------------------
1. In your new repo, click "uploading an existing file"
2. Drag ALL files from your SchoolHeat_TUKLAS folder:
   - index.html
   - style.css
   - script.js
   - manifest.json
   - service-worker.js
   - assets/ folder (with your images)
   - icons/ folder (with your icons)
3. Scroll down, type "Initial commit" in the message box
4. Click "Commit changes"

STEP 4: ENABLE GITHUB PAGES
---------------------------
1. In your repo, click "Settings" (top right tab)
2. On the left sidebar, click "Pages"
3. Under "Source", select "Deploy from a branch"
4. Branch: main, Folder: / (root)
5. Click "Save"
6. Wait 2-5 minutes
7. Your URL appears at the top: https://yourusername.github.io/schoolheat/

STEP 5: TEST YOUR PERMANENT URL
-------------------------------
1. Open the URL in your browser
2. The app should load!
3. Try it on your phone too
4. This URL is now PERMANENT — it never changes

STEP 6: CONNECT TO FIREBASE (for live data)
--------------------------------------------
See FIREBASE_SETUP.txt for instructions.

Once Firebase is connected:
- Your GitHub Pages URL shows the app
- Your laptop pushes Arduino data to Firebase
- Judges open the GitHub URL on their phones
- They see LIVE data from your Arduino!
- All wireless, all from anywhere!
